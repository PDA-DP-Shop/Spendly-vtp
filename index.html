FullScreen Link

The entire display becomes one optical data frame. Four corner anchors define the screen boundary; the cells inside carry the message. This is deliberately not a QR layout.

How to test with two phones

1. Publish this page on GitHub Pages. 2. Open it on both phones. 3. On sender tap “Show full-screen code”. 4. On receiver tap “Open camera scanner” and aim at the sender display. Keep the sender screen inside the camera guide and start at a straight-on angle.

V1 limitation: the receiver samples a regular grid, so align the screen nearly straight-on and fill the camera view. Perspective correction and robust error correction are future work. Do not use payment or private data.

What makes this different?

No central QR finder squares. The code is distributed across the whole screen, with custom corner anchors and a screen-edge frame. It is a visible full-screen code—not the earlier subtle brightness-pulsing idea.
